# Apart
Why am I going apart ?
To the city I can't fit as part.
Losing my family and friends behind,
sack filled with emotions and reverie, left behind.

I miss the dusky sunsets with a blush,
two cups of cutting-chai at crossroads with comrade, 
to get thoughts to be flush.
A puff of clouds off the skies,
vapourizing the stress and sorrow,
drifting silently to the highs.

I miss the cafe with a chattery-crowded yawn,
there sat our table at the corner,
with one queen, and the rest pieces and pawn.
Planning for the future content,contest or new conquest,
gulping down another cup of chai,
thrilling over strategies, laughter and gossips to digest.

I miss the night with a silent bang,
scavenging for supper in streets,
devouring neelanga-rice and eggs, our beloved chang.
The wise stickman craves for sweets,
sometimes melody of strings, jokes and goofy times,
licking ice cream, savoring lifes simple feats.

I miss my room with three beds and a stage,
a pug surviving on packets, moans at midnight,
sometimes for dance and witness of birthday rage.
The clever fool wrapped in blanket cocoon,
sportsman by day, anime by night,
lost in his world like hermit on the moon.

This breaks my heart in parts,
like scattered falling stars.
Recall makes my eyes numb,
as sea thunders at the shore with a hum.

For greater good got seperated,
gathering fragments,lovingly curated.
We would again be a part,
The cruel world cant keep use apart,
for we were never truly apart.
